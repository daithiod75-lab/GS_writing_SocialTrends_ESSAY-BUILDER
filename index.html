Paragraph & Sentence Structure Formula

Strict 4-Sentence Main Body Rule

To build strong, logical, and persuasive high-school level social trend essays, each essay is structured into 5 Paragraphs: Introduction, Main Body Paragraph 1, Main Body Paragraph 2, Main Body Paragraph 3, and Conclusion.
論理的で説得力のある社会事象エッセイを作成するために、各エッセイは「導入」「本論1」「本論2」「本論3」「結論」の5段落で構成されます。

🔍 The 4-Sentence Main Body Paragraph Formula (本論段落の4文ルール)

Sentence One: Topic Sentence (トピックセンテンス)
Clearly state the main topic or main idea of the paragraph. その段落で最も伝えたいメインの主張・テーマを明確に述べます。
Sentence Two: Supporting Evidence (根拠・データ)
An example from real life (statistics, quote from newspaper/book/website) which proves that the topic is true. 現実の例（統計データ、新聞・本・ウェブサイトからの引用や事実）を用いて主張を証明します。
Sentence Three: Explanation (解説・展開)
An explanation of why the second sentence supports the topic (transition words like "This shows", "As a result", "So," are essential here). なぜ第2文のデータが主張を裏付けるのかを説明します（「This shows」「As a result」などの転換語を使用）。
Sentence Four: Linking Sentence (橋渡し文)
A linking sentence: this summarizes the idea presented in the topic and makes a "bridge" to the next paragraph. 段落のアイデアを要約し、次の段落や全体の議論へスムーズにつなぐ橋渡し（ブリッジ）を行います。

📝 Official Reference Sample Main Body Paragraph

Example Breakdown

Notice how each sentence serves its designated function perfectly:

1 Sentence 1: Topic Sentence First, regular physical exercise significantly improves a student's mental focus.
2 Sentence 2: Supporting Evidence (Statistics & Quote) According to a 2024 study by the Oxford Education Board, students who walk or exercise for twenty minutes before class score 15% higher on concentration tests.
3 Sentence 3: Explanation (Transition Words) This shows that physical movement increases blood flow to the brain, which helps students stay awake and absorb information faster.
4 Sentence 4: Linking Sentence (Bridge) Because physical activity clears the mind for better learning, schools should look closely at how sports affect daily academic habits.

    📌 5-Paragraph Checklist5段落構成チェックリスト

    • Paragraph 1: Introduction (General background + Thesis statement)
    • Paragraph 2: Main Body 1 (S1 Topic, S2 Evidence/Data, S3 Explanation with "This shows...", S4 Bridge)
    • Paragraph 3: Main Body 2 (S1 Topic, S2 Evidence/Data, S3 Explanation with "As a result...", S4 Bridge)
    • Paragraph 4: Main Body 3 (S1 Topic, S2 Evidence/Data, S3 Explanation with "Consequently...", S4 Bridge)
    • Paragraph 5: Conclusion (Summary starting with "In conclusion" + Final proposal)
